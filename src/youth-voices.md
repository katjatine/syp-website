Dear Policymakers of the Scheldt,

Do you feel the pressure? The responsibility of steering the car that holds thousands, or even millions of people in it.

Maybe you do, maybe you don't. Either way, I'd like to ask you to look up. Is the city we are building harmoniously designed to manage and maintain itself, like a meandering river? Or is it rushed, controlled, and constricting, like how the Scheldt has been trapped between canal walls?

We know it's not easy. Deputy Mayor Filip Watteeuw himself told us of the horrors of angry people. Death threats, protests, court cases. You want to hear what he wishes he had done?

> Mobilizing the quiet people who agree, not fighting those who don't.

People will be angry and scared when they feel that they are losing something. So let's show them what they gain.

Max, the water engineer, agrees. He fought people for years to convince them to trust nature. To give the land back to water. And what if we change the way that we talk about nature?

What if the story we tell is not about winning the land or giving it back? What if it's inviting nature to work with us? What if we view nature not as a monster who must be... but rather as a person who has the skills and experience we need to help us.

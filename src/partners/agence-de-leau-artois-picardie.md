    text: |-
      The Agence de l'Eau Artois-Picardie is a French public body, one of six water agencies set up after France's water law of 1964. It works across the Nord and Pas-de-Calais and parts of the Somme, Aisne and Oise, which includes the French part of the Scheldt. It helps reduce water pollution and protect rivers and wetlands, with funding and with data.

      It also runs a youth parliament of its own, the Parlement des Jeunes pour l'Eau.

    heading_fr: Comment tout a commencé
    heading_nl: Hoe het begon
    text: |-
      In 1999, GoodPlanet (then called GREEN) helped organise the first European Youth Parliament for Water, in Espalion in France. The Agence was inspired and set up its own youth parliament. Young people from France and Brussels began visiting each other, and an idea grew: why not work per river basin? A river doesn't stop at a border.

      Two people brought that idea to the Scheldt: **Jo Van Cauwenberge**, director of GoodPlanet, and **Christine Dericq** of the Agence, who is responsible for its international action and youth policy. They met through the European Youth Parliament for Water and decided to create a parliament for the whole Scheldt basin.

      The Agence, GoodPlanet and the International Scheldt Commission signed their cooperation in 2007, and in October 2008 the Scheldt Youth Parliament held its first plenary, in Antwerp.

Des jeunes de France et de Bruxelles ont commencé à se rendre visite, et une idée a germé : pourquoi ne pas travailler par bassin versant ? Un fleuve ne s'arrête pas à une frontière.

      Deux personnes ont porté cette idée jusqu'à l'Escaut : **Jo Van Cauwenberge**, directeur de GoodPlanet, et **Christine Dericq**, chargée de l'action internationale et de la politique jeunesse à l'Agence. Ils se sont rencontrés grâce au Parlement européen de la Jeunesse pour l'Eau et ont décidé de créer un parlement pour tout le bassin de l'Escaut.

      L'Agence, GoodPlanet et la Commission Internationale de l'Escaut ont signé leur coopération en 2007, et en octobre 2008, le Parlement des Jeunes de l'Escaut a tenu sa première séance plénière, à Anvers.

    text: |-
      - **2011, Douai.** We prepared the first World Youth Parliament for Water. The fresco we made there went to Marseille, and then found a place in the Agence's offices.
      - **2012, Marseille.** The Agence, GoodPlanet and the International Secretariat for Water organised that first World Youth Parliament for Water at the World Water Forum.
      - **2016, Marrakesh.** At the COP22 climate summit, our members signed an agreement with the new youth assembly of the Sebou in Morocco. The Agence signed one with the Sebou's water agency.
      - **2023, Lille.** The Parlement des Jeunes pour l'Eau turned twenty, and two of our members presented their projects at the celebration.
      - **2024 to 2026.** The Agence co-funded our latest group, alongside Erasmus+.

      The Agence is also the point of contact for participants from France.

Thirteen young people spent three days at the Kanoa outdoor centre in Wolphaartsdijk, hosted by HZ University of Applied Sciences. They played the Scheldt game, discussed legal rights for the river and kayaked on the Veerse Meer. Keynote speaker Ad Faasse: "We must give young people a stage in such a way that it inspires others to do similar things."

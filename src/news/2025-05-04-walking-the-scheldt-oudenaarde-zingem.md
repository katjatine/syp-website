Our second walk followed the Maarkebeek and the Zwalm, through the old town of Oudenaarde and Ename. The group met a citizens' group, local guides and the secretary general of the International Scheldt Commission. The mayor and deputy mayors of Kruisem, which includes Zingem, gave them a warm welcome.

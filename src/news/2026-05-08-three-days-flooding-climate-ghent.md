Fourteen participants met in Ghent for the group's last exchange, on flooding, climate adaptation and citizen participation. Filip Watteeuw, deputy mayor of Ghent, talked about the human side of redesigning a city. Max Verdonck of De Vlaamse Waterweg explained the flood defences. On Saturday the group cycled out to see them.
